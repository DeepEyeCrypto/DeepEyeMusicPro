# Privacy Policy for DeepEyeMusicPro

**Last Updated:** May 2026

DeepEyeMusicPro ("the App") is developed by DeepEye ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the App.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide
- **Search Queries**: When you search for music, your search query is sent to third-party services (YouTube) to retrieve results. We do not store your search history on any server.
- **Playback Preferences**: Audio equalizer settings and UI preferences are stored locally on your device only.

### 1.2 Information We Do NOT Collect
- We do **not** collect personal identification information (name, email, phone number).
- We do **not** track your location.
- We do **not** use advertising SDKs or ad networks.
- We do **not** collect analytics or usage telemetry.
- We do **not** share any data with third parties for marketing purposes.

### 1.3 Automatically Collected Information
- **Crash Reports**: If you opt in, crash reports (device model, Android version, stack trace) may be collected to improve app stability. No personally identifiable information is included.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information solely to:
- Provide music search and playback functionality.
- Store your local preferences (equalizer, theme, playback settings).
- Improve app stability through anonymized crash reports (opt-in only).

## 3. Data Storage

- All user data (favorites, playlists, settings) is stored **locally on your device**.
- We do not operate any backend servers that store user data.
- Downloaded music files are stored in your device's local storage and are accessible only to the App.

## 4. Third-Party Services

The App connects to the following third-party services:

| Service | Purpose | Data Sent |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| YouTube (google.com) | Music search & streaming | Search queries, video IDs |
| Google Cast | Wireless audio casting | Playback metadata |

These services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them:
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 5. Permissions

The App requests the following permissions:

| Permission | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| Internet | Music streaming and search |
| Foreground Service | Background audio playback |
| Bluetooth | Wireless audio device connection |
| Notifications | Playback controls notification |
| Audio Settings | Sound equalizer and effects |
| Read Media Audio | Access local music library |

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

## 7. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:
- All network connections use HTTPS/TLS encryption.
- Sensitive credentials (if any) are stored using Android Keystore.
- Native code is hardened against buffer overflows and memory corruption.
- No cleartext traffic is permitted.

## 8. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access**: View all data stored by the App (available in Settings).
- **Delete**: Clear all app data through Android Settings > Apps > DeepEyeMusicPro > Clear Data.
- **Opt Out**: Disable crash reporting in the App settings.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
